# Inquiry Email Notifications

Contact form submissions are saved at `/admin/inquiries` and emailed to **info@steerbuilderscorporation.com**.

Emails go to the **office inbox**, not the customer’s address on the form.

## First-time activation

The first notification may be an activation email from FormSubmit. Open **info@steerbuilderscorporation.com** (check spam), click **Activate Form**, then submit the contact form again.
